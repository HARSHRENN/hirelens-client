Questions

What people ask before they sign up.

Something else on your mind? Open the demo workspace and look around with sample data.

How long is the free trial?

Every new workspace starts with a free trial of up to 7 days with every feature switched on. Sign-up needs your name, work email and a username, and no card.

What happens when the trial ends?

You move to one monthly plan per workspace. The invoice arrives by email with the tax shown, and your jobs and candidates stay exactly where they are.

Can other recruiters see my candidates?

No. Every account gets its own private workspace, and the database keeps one account's jobs and candidates invisible to every other account. You can export everything as a backup any time.

Which resume files can I upload?

PDF, Word (DOCX and DOC), plain text, RTF and Markdown, plus photos or scans in PNG, JPG and WEBP. Drop up to sixty at once on a job.

Does it send interview emails for me?

Yes. When you schedule an interview, the invite goes out with a calendar file and the panel copied in, and a reminder goes out on the morning of the interview. Any message can also open as a WhatsApp chat or a Gmail draft.

Do I need to install anything?

No. HireLens runs in the browser on a laptop or a phone. Open the demo to look around with sample data first.
