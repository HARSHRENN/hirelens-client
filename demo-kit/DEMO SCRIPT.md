# HireLens demo, about 15 minutes

Laptop, Chrome, full screen. Start on the website, end on the dashboard.

1. **Website** (1 min). Scroll slowly through the hero, the dashboard card straightening, the features and How it works. Click **Open the app**.
2. **Admin** (1 min). Open /admin. Show the API keys (AI, WhatsApp, Resend email, Google Maps, automation webhook) and the match weights. Fill in Company name and Recruiter name. Press Test AI connection.
3. **Create the job** (1 min). Jobs → New job. Paste "JD - Senior Java Developer.txt" into the description box and press **Read pasted JD**. The fields fill themselves. Create the job.
4. **Drop resumes** (2 min). Profiles tab, keep "Read with AI" on. Drag in files 01 to 06 and 08 together. Point out that PDF, Word, TXT, RTF and a phone photo of a resume are all read. Show the ranked table: match %, experience, CTC, notice and distance from Malad. Shortlisted rows carry the lime bar.
5. **Duplicate check** (20 s). Drop "07 Priya Nair - updated resume.pdf". It updates Priya's profile instead of adding a second row.
6. **Open a candidate** (2 min). Click Rohan Mehta and show the match card: score, five weighted parts, strengths, gaps, skill chips. Message tab: pick the WhatsApp template, Open in WhatsApp. Interview tab: pick L1, Open in Google Calendar, paste a Meet link, Save. Remarks tab: add "Spoke at 4pm, interested".
7. **Calibration call** (1 min). Job → Calibration call. Paste "Calibration call notes (Hinglish).txt" and press Summarise into requirement. Kafka becomes a must-have and the budget goes to 26 LPA. Apply to job, then press Re-score on Profiles. Translate the summary into Marathi.
8. **Ideal candidate** (30 s). Build the ideal profile from the shortlist and show "Closest not yet shortlisted".
9. **Share the job** (30 s). Share job tab: LinkedIn and X open with the post filled in, and the 1080 x 1080 Instagram creative is ready to save.
10. **Pipeline and dashboard** (1 min). Drag two cards to L1 and L2 and one to Selected. Dashboard: totals, screening levels, profiles by technology, upcoming interviews. Tracker: export CSV.
11. **Tracker ticks and client sharing** (1 min). Tracker: tick three candidates and untick the Phone and Email columns. Press Share with client, choose Client format. The zip holds the tracker plus resumes with contacts hidden and your agency watermark. Show WhatsApp all / Email all on the ticked rows.
12. **Candidate depth** (1 min). Open Rohan Mehta: employment timeline by year, current company, companies worked. Interviews tab: L1 and L2 feedback from panelists, L3 upcoming. Self rating tab: the candidate's own % against the JD.
13. **Panelists and Admin** (1 min). Panelists: interviews taken, selects, rejects, average rating; click a name for their feedback. Admin: recruiter analytics (screened to joined/declined), panelist analytics, add an L4 level, match colours (green 70+, orange 40-69, red under 40).
14. **Dashboard periods and billing** (1 min). Dashboard: switch Weekly / Monthly, pick From-To dates, click Offered or Joined. Billing: the trial countdown, then End trial now (demo): the invoice appears with product + 18% tax = final, and downloads as a PDF.

Before the meeting: add the AI key in Admin and run steps 3 to 5 once on that network, so you know it works. Then delete it from Admin → Data and redo it live, or keep it and show the result.
